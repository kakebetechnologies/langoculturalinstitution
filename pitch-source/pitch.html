01 · Why this, why now

Culture lives in people, and most of it was never written down.

Uganda has more than 50 indigenous communities and around 40 living languages. Their proverbs, clans, histories and songs are carried in memory and spoken from one generation to the next, and that chain is under strain.



Knowledge is oral and fragile

Languages, clans and stories are held by elders. When one passes, a library closes, and little of it is recorded or searchable.



Young people and the diaspora are far from home

Many have no easy way to learn the language, find their clan or hear the stories their grandparents know.



Our languages are missing from AI

Most Ugandan languages have almost no digital text or speech data, so translators, voice assistants and learning tools do not work in them.



Institutions lack the tools

Cultural institutions hold the mandate and the trust of their people, but not the software to gather, check and share what the community knows.

Our answer

One app that every community can own.

Lango App puts a community’s dictionary, clans, heritage, history, radio, podcast and Bible in one phone app and lets its people add what they know. Its own language experts review every entry.

  • Preserves language, clans, history and oral tradition
  • Collects words, proverbs, stories, audio and photos from the community
  • Connects people of the same clan and culture, at home and abroad
  • Teaches the next generation, a few minutes a day
  • Feeds AI with consented, expert-checked language data

04 · Data and AI

From a phone in the village to data that teaches AI.

Every word, proverb and story added to the app can become part of a clean, consented, expert-checked dataset that belongs to the community. That is what low-resource language AI is missing.

What the data can power


Machine translation

Local language to English and back, from verified word and sentence pairs.



Speech recognition

Voice to text for languages that today have almost no recordings.



Text to speech

Natural voices for learning, radio and accessibility.



Assistants and learning tools

Chat, search and teaching tools that answer in the community’s own language.

 Responsible by design

  • Consent first. Each contribution carries the contributor’s choice: keep ownership, share openly with credit, or give it to the institution.
  • Experts in the loop. Nothing is published, or used as data, before the community’s language experts review it.
  • Community ownership. Each institution governs its own data and decides who may use it.
  • Privacy. Names show as first name and initial, and members can delete their account.
TodayA dictionary-based translator and assistant that answer from the verified archive, plus the contribution and review workflow that produces the data.
Next, with research partnersTrain and evaluate translation and speech models on the verified data, and return them to the app and to the community.

05 · Beyond Lango

Built for Lango. Ready for every community in Uganda.

Lango is where we test and prove the idea. The platform is designed so that Buganda, Acholi, Teso, Ankole and every other community can have its own edition: its own name, language, clans and content, on shared technology.

Examples of communities the platform is built to serve. A prototype Acholi edition has already been built from the same code base.

 Shared by every edition

  • The app, admin panel and API
  • Contribution, review and verification workflow
  • Dictionary, translator, quizzes, clan groups
  • Radio, podcast and Bible player
  • The AI data pipeline and its safeguards

 Owned by each community

  • Name, logo and colours
  • Language, and the experts who verify it
  • Clan or chiefdom register, districts, history
  • All content and all data it collects
  • Its administrators and its institution’s voice
How a new edition launches
1. PartnerA cultural institution or foundation sponsors its edition
2. ConfigureName, colours, language, clans and districts
3. Fill and verifySeed content, train editors, start expert review
4. LaunchRelease on Android with the community
