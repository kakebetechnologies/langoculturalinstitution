# 16. Measuring Success

*How the Foundation will know the platform is working: a short set of indicators, linked to the objectives in Chapter 2 and reviewed every quarter.*

## 16.1 Principles

- **Few, meaningful measures.** We prefer ten that the Foundation actually uses to twenty that nobody reads.
- **Built into the platform.** The admin dashboard shows most of these numbers without extra tools.
- **Targets set with the Foundation.** The baselines and targets below are agreed in discovery; we do not impose numbers we cannot defend.
- **Privacy first.** Measures count activity, not individuals.

## 16.3 Review rhythm

- **Monthly:** the Foundation's coordinator reviews the dashboard.
- **Quarterly:** a review meeting with Kakebe covers the indicators, what content is missing, what users ask the assistant that it cannot answer, and the roadmap for the next quarter.
- **Annually:** a report to leadership and partners, written in plain language, that can also be shared with donors.

## 16.4 What good looks like in twelve months

By the end of the first year we want the Foundation to be able to say, with evidence:

1. The **Lango language, clans, districts and heritage** are documented in a trusted, sourced and growing public archive.
2. **Young Langos** use the app to learn and to contribute, and **elders' knowledge** is being recorded with consent.
3. The **diaspora** is connected, informed and giving.
4. The Foundation's **own team runs the platform**, supported by a clear, affordable plan.
5. Partners, donors, schools and media treat the platform as a **reference source**.
