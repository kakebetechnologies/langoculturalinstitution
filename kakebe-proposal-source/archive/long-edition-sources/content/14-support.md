# 14. Support, Maintenance and Service Levels

*A launched platform needs care. Here is what is included after go-live, the support plans on offer, and the response times the Foundation can expect.*

## 14.1 Hypercare: the first 90 days are included

For the first 90 days after go-live we watch the platform closely and fix problems fast.

- **Daily monitoring** of availability, errors and performance.
- **Rapid fixes** for defects found by real use, at no extra charge.
- **Usage review** at 30, 60 and 90 days: what people use, where they struggle, which content is missing.
- **Training clinics** for staff and moderators.
- **Hypercare report** and a handover to the chosen support plan.

## 14.4 What maintenance covers

| Area | Activities |
|---|---|
| **Security** | Operating system and software updates, dependency updates, certificate renewal, security monitoring |
| **Reliability** | Uptime monitoring, error tracking, backups, restore tests, capacity checks |
| **Performance** | Query and image optimisation, caching reviews, load checks before big events |
| **Mobile** | Compatibility with new Android and iOS versions, store policy changes, over-the-air updates for small fixes |
| **Content platform** | Fixes to import tools, translations screen and moderation workflow |
| **Data** | Retention schedule, export on request, privacy requests |

## 14.5 Releases and change

- **Regular releases** on a published schedule, with release notes in plain language.
- **Emergency fixes** released immediately when needed.
- **A versioned API** so that the app keeps working when the website is updated.
- **A shared roadmap** reviewed each quarter: new features, improvements and the roadmap items in Section 6.9.

## 14.6 Continuity and exit

We want the Foundation to stay with Kakebe because the service is good, not because it is trapped.

- The Foundation owns the code, content, data, domain, hosting accounts and store accounts.
- Documentation and a deployment runbook are handed over, so another competent developer can take over.
- Content and data can be exported at any time in standard formats (CSV, JSON, SQL).
- On termination of a support plan, Kakebe provides an orderly handover of credentials and knowledge within [TBC: for example 30 days].
