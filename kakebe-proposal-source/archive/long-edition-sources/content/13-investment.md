# 13. Investment and Commercial Options

*Three clear packages, a transparent breakdown, honest running costs and milestone payments tied to deliverables the Foundation can see.*

> **Draft pricing.** All amounts below are to be completed by Kakebe and confirmed with the Foundation after the discovery workshop. Currency is Ugandan shillings (UGX). The structure, not the numbers, is what this draft settles.

## 13.1 How we price

- **Fixed price per package,** agreed after discovery, so the Foundation knows the total before work starts.
- **Itemised.** Each major deliverable is priced separately, so the Foundation can see where the money goes and choose what to include.
- **Milestone payments** tied to approvals, not to the calendar (Section 13.5).
- **Honest running costs.** The one-time build cost and the annual cost of running the platform are shown side by side.
- **No hidden extras.** What is not included is stated in Chapter 17.

## 13.7 Ways to fund and sustain the platform

A cultural institution should not carry the whole cost alone. The platform itself can help, and we are happy to support the Foundation in preparing for any of the following.

| Source | How the platform helps |
|---|---|
| **Grants for digital cultural preservation** | A funded, accountable digital archive with a transparency dashboard is an attractive proposition for partners and development agencies |
| **Diaspora giving and sponsorship** | The donation, pledge and sponsor-a-programme flow reaches Langos abroad directly |
| **Event ticketing** | Festival and cultural event tickets, printable now and with QR check-in as an option |
| **Partnerships** | Visible programme pages and partner listings for businesses and institutions |
| **Memberships (future)** | Clan or supporter memberships, once live payments are enabled |

We can include a short **sustainability plan** in the discovery deliverables so that the Foundation has a clear view of running costs and income options before it commits.
