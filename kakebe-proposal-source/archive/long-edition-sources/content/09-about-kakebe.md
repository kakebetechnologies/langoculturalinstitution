# 9. About Kakebe Technologies

*A youth-led technology company in Lira City that builds software, AI and robotics for real local problems, and trains the next generation to build them too.*

## 9.1 Who we are

Kakebe Technologies Limited is a youth-led technology hub based in **Lira City**, Uganda, in the heart of Lango. We build software, AI tools and robotics that solve real local problems, and we train young people to build the next ones themselves. For more than five years we have worked with non-profits, government offices, schools, churches, businesses and community groups across Northern Uganda and beyond.

## 9.4 Our programmes

Kakebe does not only deliver projects. It builds capacity in the community where the Foundation lives.

- **Kakebe Tech Camp.** A residential bootcamp for ages 13 to 30 in robotics, AI and machine learning, web and app development, entrepreneurship, engineering and finance, and multimedia. Camp 2025 trained 257 young people from across East Africa, who left with working prototypes. The next camp runs on 14 to 23 December 2026.
- **Digital Bridge Internship Programme.** Interns in IT, business and animation work alongside our engineers, designers and marketers on live projects and graduate with a certificate and a portfolio.
- **Innovation Programme.** Mentorship, technical support and incubation for young innovators, including the **Lango Innovation Challenge** and regular Tech Talks. Several of our own products began here.

For the Foundation these programmes are an asset: a pool of trained local talent, a route for interns to contribute to the platform under supervision, and a platform story that inspires the next generation.

## 9.6 Why Kakebe is the right partner for the Foundation

1. **We are local.** Our office is in Lira City. We can sit with elders, clan leaders and language experts in person, run workshops in Lira and visit the districts, without flights, time zones or invoices for travel from abroad.
2. **We have already built it.** The demonstrator in Appendix B is a working version of what this proposal describes. The Foundation can see and test before committing.
3. **We have proven delivery across the same technologies.** Content-managed websites, multi-role admin systems, mobile apps on a shared API, offline-first design, mobile-money payments, event ticketing, tracking codes and government work: each appears in our portfolio (Chapter 10).
4. **We build capacity, not dependency.** The Foundation's staff are trained to run the platform, the documentation is complete, and the code, content and data belong to the Foundation.
5. **We keep the money and the skills in Lango.** The work is done in Lira by a Lira-based team and local trainees, which is consistent with a cultural institution's mission.
6. **We are accountable.** Four clear stages, fixed milestones, a staging site to review at any time, and a named team (Chapter 11).
