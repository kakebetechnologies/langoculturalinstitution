## 18.2 Questions for the discovery workshop

To use the 90 minutes well, we suggest the Foundation consider these in advance.

1. Which **package** (A, B or C) best fits the Foundation's priorities and budget?
2. Who will be the **project coordinator**, and who will sit on the **language committee** and **history and heritage panel**?
3. What **existing content** is there (registers, dictionaries, histories, photographs, recordings), and who owns and can approve it?
4. What are the **cultural protocols** for restricted knowledge, recordings of elders and photographs of children?
5. What **identity** will the platform carry: name, logo, colours, domain name?
6. How should **leadership and officeholders** be presented, and who approves changes to those pages?
7. Which **recognition features** (points, levels, leaderboard) should be public?
8. Is **live payment** (mobile money and cards) wanted at launch or later?
9. What are the **key dates** (festivals, anniversaries, funding deadlines) the launch should align with?
10. Which **partners, donors or institutions** should be involved or informed?
