# 17. Assumptions, Dependencies and Exclusions

*The conditions on which the timeline and price in this proposal depend, so that there are no surprises on either side.*

## 17.1 Assumptions

| # | Assumption |
|---|---|
| 1 | The Foundation nominates a project coordinator and the roles in Section 12.2 within the first week |
| 2 | The Foundation provides feedback and approvals within **five working days** of each request; delays extend the timeline by the same time |
| 3 | The Foundation supplies its logo, brand colours and photographs, or agrees a licensed alternative, by the end of Phase 0 |
| 4 | Cultural, historical and language content is supplied or verified by the Foundation's own experts; Kakebe structures, loads and formats it |
| 5 | The platform is delivered in **English and Leb Lango**; further languages are quoted separately |
| 6 | Hosting is on a reputable provider with HTTPS; the Foundation holds the domain, hosting and store accounts |
| 7 | Live payment collection (mobile money and cards) is an **optional upgrade**, not part of the first release, unless the Foundation selects it |
| 8 | The scope matches the demonstrator, adapted to the Foundation's identity and requirements; materially different requirements are handled through change control |
| 9 | The Foundation's legal adviser reviews the Privacy and Terms pages, which we draft |
| 10 | The pilot involves 20 to 40 users and is arranged by the Foundation with Kakebe's help |

## 17.2 Dependencies on the Foundation

- Named people for each role, available for scheduled reviews.
- Access to existing content, registers and documents, with permission to digitise them.
- Decisions on cultural protocols, restricted content, public recognition features and the Cultural Review Panel.
- Store accounts (Google Play Console, Apple Developer Programme) and a domain name, in the Foundation's name.
- Legal review of privacy notice, terms and contributor licence.
- A launch communication plan on the Foundation's channels.

## 17.3 Exclusions

The following are not in the price unless added by written agreement:

- Writing, researching or translating original cultural or historical content. (We can recommend people and processes, but the Foundation's authorities own this work.)
- Professional photography, audio or video production, and large-scale scanning beyond Option C's scope.
- Live payment integration and payment gateway fees.
- SMS and USSD services, an open community forum, and the other roadmap items in Section 6.9.
- Printed materials, merchandise and event production.
- Legal advice, including data protection registration.
- Hardware, devices and internet connectivity for the Foundation's offices.
- Third-party fees such as hosting, domains, store accounts and SMS credits, which are paid by the Foundation at cost.

## 17.4 Intellectual property and confidentiality

- **Content and data** belong to the Foundation. Contributors keep authorship of what they submit and grant the Foundation a licence as stated on the contribution form.
- **Custom code** written for the Foundation is licensed to the Foundation without time limit for its own use and modification [TBC: final licence wording to be agreed with the Foundation's legal adviser].
- **Kakebe's pre-existing components** (including the foundation code of the platform and general-purpose libraries) remain Kakebe's property and are licensed to the Foundation for use with the platform.
- **Third-party and open-source components** remain under their own licences, which are listed in the handover documentation.
- **Confidentiality.** Each party keeps the other's confidential information private; personal data is handled under Section 8.5.

## 17.5 Acceptance

A deliverable is accepted when it meets its documented acceptance criteria, has passed testing with no critical or high defects open, and is signed off by the Foundation's coordinator. If the Foundation does not respond within [TBC: for example 10 working days] of a delivery notice, Kakebe will escalate to the executive sponsor before treating the deliverable as accepted.
