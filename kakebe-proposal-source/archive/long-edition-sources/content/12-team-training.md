## 12.4 Governance and communication

| Forum | Frequency | Purpose | Attendees |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Steering meeting** | Monthly | Progress, budget, risks, strategic decisions | Foundation sponsor and coordinator, Kakebe lead and CTO |
| **Project call** | Weekly, 30 minutes | Status, blockers, decisions | Coordinator, Kakebe lead |
| **Cycle demonstration** | Every two weeks | See the work, give feedback | Coordinator, panel members, Kakebe team |
| **Content review sessions** | As scheduled | Review language and heritage content | Panels, Kakebe |
| **Status report** | Weekly, one page | Done, next, risks, decisions | Shared by email |

**Escalation.** Issues are raised first with the project coordinator and the Kakebe lead; unresolved issues go to the steering group within three working days.

## 12.5 Training and capacity building

The Foundation should be able to run the platform on its own after launch. Training is therefore part of the scope, not an extra.

| Audience | Sessions | Format and length | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Administrators** | Platform overview, roles and users, settings, backups, exports | 1 day, hands-on, in Lira | Can manage users, settings and data |
| **Editors and communications** | News, pages, events, media, translations, SEO basics | 1 day, hands-on | Can publish and maintain content |
| **Moderators and language experts** | Review queue, contribution status, dictionary and proverbs, translations | 1 day, hands-on | Can review and approve contributions with confidence |
| **Clan leaders** | Clan self-service, how to correct and add information | Half day, in groups, in English and Leb Lango | Can maintain their clan page |
| **Youth ambassadors** | Assisted contribution: helping elders record stories with consent | Half day | Can record and submit on behalf of elders |
| **Leadership** | Dashboards, reports, what the platform shows | 2 hours | Can use the platform for decisions and communication |

**Materials.** An administrator manual, short video tutorials, quick-reference cards in English and Leb Lango, a moderator checklist, a style guide for entries, and a recorded training library in the admin help area.

**Training of trainers.** Two or three Foundation staff learn in depth, so that new staff and volunteers can be trained without Kakebe.

**Post-launch clinics.** Drop-in sessions, in person or online, during hypercare and at the first quarterly review.

## 12.6 Handover and documentation

At handover the Foundation receives:

- The **source code** and a documented deployment process, with the Foundation as owner.
- **Access** to hosting, domain, database, store accounts and push services, in the Foundation's name.
- **Technical documentation**: architecture, data model, API reference, deployment and restore instructions.
- **User documentation**: admin manual, moderator guide and training videos.
- A **content inventory** and a record of the sources behind every loaded fact.
- A **maintenance runbook** covering backups, updates and what to do when something goes wrong.
