## A.6 Roadmap beyond the first release

| ID | Feature | Value |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | SMS and USSD fallback | Reach on basic phones and without data |
| R2 | One-time-code (OTP) sign-in | Easier sign-in for first-time users |
| R3 | Community forum and clan discussion groups | A moderated space for clans and the diaspora |
| R4 | Maps and 360 degree tours inside the app | Brings places to life on the phone |
| R5 | Extended AI assistant, still limited to reviewed content | A richer way to ask questions |
| R6 | Kiosks and school and museum displays | Brings the archive to places without phones |
