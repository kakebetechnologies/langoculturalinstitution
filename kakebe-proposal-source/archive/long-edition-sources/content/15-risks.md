# 15. Risks and Mitigation

*What could go wrong, how likely it is, how much it would matter, and what we will do about it before it does.*

## 15.1 Risk register

We keep a living risk register throughout the project and review it at every weekly call and monthly steering meeting. These are the principal risks at the start.

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Disputed or inaccurate cultural content** damages trust | Medium | High | Source citations; trust labels; Cultural Review Panel; nothing published without approval; officeholders shown exactly as the Foundation confirms | Foundation sponsor |
| 2 | **Content arrives late or incomplete,** delaying launch | High | Medium | Content audit in week 1; launch with a defined minimum content set; bulk import tools; supervised interns; content treated as a work stream with owners | Foundation coordinator |
| 3 | **Low connectivity** limits use | High | Medium | Offline-first app, versioned offline dictionary, lite web mode, queued uploads | Kakebe technical lead |
| 4 | **Limited stakeholder availability** for reviews | Medium | High | Named roles and time commitments in week 1; scheduled review sessions; two-week demonstrations; decision log | Both coordinators |
| 5 | **Low adoption** after launch | Medium | High | Launch plan, ambassadors, clan leader onboarding, events, recognition, regular content (word of the day, news); success measures reviewed quarterly | Foundation communications, Kakebe |
| 6 | **Spam, abuse or inappropriate uploads** | Medium | Medium | Moderation before publication; rate limits; account controls; report routes | Moderators |
| 7 | **Privacy breach or data loss** | Low | High | Security controls and review; encrypted connections; backups with restore tests; data protection design and training | Kakebe technical lead |
| 8 | **Scope creep** | Medium | Medium | Agreed scope; single backlog; change control; roadmap for later phases | Kakebe project lead |
| 9 | **Key-person dependency** on either side | Medium | Medium | Named deputies; documentation; training of trainers | Both |
| 10 | **App store delays or rejection** | Medium | Medium | Early account setup; store guideline review; web app available from day one; Android first | Kakebe mobile lead |
| 11 | **Payment integration delays** (if enabled) | Medium | Low | Treated as an optional upgrade; pledges recorded manually in the first release | Kakebe project lead |
| 12 | **Hosting or infrastructure failure** | Low | High | Reputable hosting, monitoring, daily off-site backups, restore plan | Kakebe technical lead |
| 13 | **Sensitive or restricted knowledge shared inappropriately** | Low | High | Restricted-record setting, consent capture, protocol agreed with the Foundation, review tiers | Cultural Review Panel |
| 14 | **Sustainability: running costs unfunded** | Medium | Medium | Running costs shown openly (Section 13.4); funding options (Section 13.7); support plans sized to budget | Foundation sponsor |
| 15 | **Leadership or institutional changes** during the project | Medium | Medium | Governance by role, not individual; written decisions; neutral presentation of officeholders | Foundation sponsor |

## 15.2 How we manage risk

- **Early visibility.** A staging site and a two-week demonstration rhythm mean problems surface in days, not months.
- **Decisions in writing.** A decision log records what was decided, by whom and when.
- **Escalation.** Risks are escalated within three working days if they are not resolved at project level.
- **Contingency.** A contingency line in the budget (Chapter 13) covers the unexpected without a dispute.
