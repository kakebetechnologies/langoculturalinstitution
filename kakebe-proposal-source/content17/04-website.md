# 4. The Public Website

*A fast, bilingual, brand-led home for the Foundation's history, language, clans and heritage, designed to be read on a phone first.*

The site carries the Foundation's own identity: logo, colours, photography and tone. It is mobile first, switches between English and Leb Lango in one tap, targets WCAG 2.1 AA, and has a lightweight mode for slow connections.

| Section | What visitors find |
|---|---|
| **Home, About, Leadership** | Quick actions, word of the day, mission, the Won Nyaci, cabinet and elders |
| **History** | Ten chapters with a table of contents, timeline, glossary and a list of sources |
| **Districts and Lira City** | Atlas, comparison table, map, and a page per district (people, economy, places, history) |
| **Clans and notable Langos** | A searchable Clan Explorer with a page per clan (totem, district, leader); biographies of notable people |
| **Heritage and biodiversity** | Ceremonies, attire and crafts, music and dance, symbols, sites on a map; a species register with a sightings map |
| **Language Hub** | Dictionary, English to Leb Lango translator, proverbs, greetings, lessons, "suggest a word" |
| **Library and media** | Sourced documents with downloads; photo, video, audio and podcast galleries; 360 degree tours |
| **News, events, programmes** | Bilingual news, event registration with printable ticket, programmes with progress and a sponsor button |
| **Get involved and giving** | Contribute, volunteer, scholarship, grant, partner, clan registration, diaspora corner; donation pledges and a transparency page |
| **Assistant, search, open data** | A question box that answers only from reviewed content, with sources; faceted search; documented open data |

## The Language Hub

Search works in both directions and folds special letters, so typing *e* finds *ε* and *ng* finds *ŋ* on a plain keyboard. The translator uses the dictionary, not machine translation, and says so. Anyone can suggest a word or proverb: a duplicate check runs, a language expert edits and approves, and the entry appears with a **Community** label until verified. The demonstrator already holds a starter dictionary of 2,829 entries, every one marked *unverified* until the Foundation's experts review it.

## Community participation

One simple form lets a visitor contribute a cultural observation, natural heritage sighting, word, proverb, oral history, document or heritage site, with GPS, photos, audio and files, and a **tracking code** to follow it. Accounts earn points, levels and badges. A **clan picker** on every registration, with a "my clan is not listed" option that alerts the administrators, builds the register over time. Search-engine friendly pages, share previews and a newsletter list are included; privacy-conscious analytics are added in this engagement.
