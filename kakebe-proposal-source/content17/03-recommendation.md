# 3. One Platform, Three Surfaces

*Why the website and the mobile app belong together, and how one shared platform gives the Foundation more reach for less effort.*

A single platform with **one backend and three surfaces**: a public website for reach, credibility and search; an admin control panel where the Foundation's staff run everything; and the Lango Mobile App for daily use, offline learning and field contribution.

![fig|Figure 3.1: One platform, three surfaces. Every surface talks to the same JSON API, database and review queue.](figures/fig-platform.svg)

## Why not the website alone, or the app alone?

| Need | Website | Mobile app |
|---|---|---|
| Be found on Google, shared in a link, cited by media | **Strong.** Every page has its own address and share preview | Weak |
| Read long histories, tables and maps; staff administration | **Strong** on desktop and tablet | Short reading only; not for admin |
| Work with no internet | Limited (cached pages) | **Strong.** Offline dictionary, queued contributions, saved tickets |
| Record a voice, photo or GPS point at the source | Possible, clumsy | **Strong.** Native camera, microphone, location |
| Remind and re-engage people | Email only | **Strong.** Push notifications, word of the day |
| People who will not install an app | **Strong.** Nothing to install | Installable web app (PWA) covers them |

The website is the Foundation's **public face and control room**; the app is its **daily companion and field recorder**. Together they cover every audience.

## What "shared" means

**One set of accounts** (sign in once, on either). **One content store** (a history chapter published in the admin is the same chapter the app shows, in English and Leb Lango). **One review queue** (every contribution, word or proverb lands in the same moderation workspace with the same status model and notifications). **One API**, which any future channel (a kiosk, SMS, a partner portal) can also use. A correction made once is corrected everywhere.

Building the three together costs far less than building them one after the other. If the Foundation prefers to phase delivery, the website and admin can launch first and the app follow, with nothing rebuilt; the scope options and their cost are in the separate Financial Proposal.
