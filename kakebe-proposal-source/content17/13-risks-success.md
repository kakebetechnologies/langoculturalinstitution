# 13. Risks, Success Measures and Assumptions

*What could go wrong and what we do about it, how the Foundation will know the platform is working, and the conditions the three-month plan depends on.*

| Risk | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Disputed or inaccurate cultural content** damages trust | Sources shown, trust labels, Cultural Review Panel, nothing published without approval, officeholders shown as the Foundation confirms | Foundation sponsor |
| **Content arrives late or incomplete** | Content audit in week 1, minimum launch set agreed in week 2, bulk import tools, supervised interns | Foundation coordinator |
| **Reviews are slow** (a three-month plan has no slack) | Named roles in week 1, five-working-day review rule, fortnightly demonstrations, decision log | Both coordinators |
| **Low connectivity** limits use | Offline-first app, versioned offline dictionary, lite web mode, queued uploads | Kakebe technical lead |
| **Low adoption** after launch | Launch plan, youth ambassadors, clan-leader onboarding, events, recognition, regular content | Foundation communications |
| **Spam, abuse, privacy breach, data loss** | Moderation before publication, rate limits, security review and penetration test, encrypted connections, backups with restore tests | Kakebe technical lead |
| **App-store delay or rejection** | Early account set-up, guideline review, web app live from day one, Android first | Kakebe mobile lead |
| **Scope creep** | Agreed scope, single backlog, change control, a roadmap for later phases | Kakebe project lead |
| **Running costs unfunded, leadership changes** | Costs shown openly in the Financial Proposal; governance by role, not individual; neutral presentation of officeholders | Foundation sponsor |

## Measuring success

A short set of indicators, linked to the seven objectives, shown on the admin dashboard and reviewed monthly by the coordinator and quarterly with Kakebe; baselines and targets are agreed at discovery [TBC]. They count activity, not individuals: **records in the archive** and the **share marked verified**; **oral histories** captured; **dictionary searches** and offline downloads; **members** by district, clan and country; **monthly active users** and installs; **contributions per month** and the median time to a decision; registrations, tickets and giving; staff who can run the platform unaided; uptime.

## Assumptions, dependencies and exclusions

- The Foundation names its coordinator and panels in week 1, gives feedback within **five working days**, supplies its logo, colours and photographs by the end of week 2, and arranges the pilot of 20 to 40 users.
- Cultural, historical and language content is supplied or verified by the Foundation's experts; Kakebe structures, loads and formats it. The platform is delivered in **English and Leb Lango**; other languages are quoted separately.
- Store accounts (Google Play, Apple Developer), the domain and hosting are in the Foundation's name; its legal adviser reviews the privacy notice, terms and contributor licence.
- **Not included unless agreed in writing:** writing or translating original cultural content, professional photography or video, large-scale scanning, live payment integration and gateway fees, SMS and USSD, an open forum, printed materials, legal advice, and third-party fees (hosting, domain, store accounts, SMS credits) paid by the Foundation at cost.
- **Intellectual property:** content and data belong to the Foundation; contributors keep authorship and grant a licence; custom code is licensed to the Foundation without time limit [TBC: final wording with the Foundation's legal adviser]; Kakebe's pre-existing components and open-source libraries stay under their own licences. Each party keeps the other's confidential information private.
- A deliverable is **accepted** when it meets its documented criteria with no critical or high defects open and the coordinator signs it off.
