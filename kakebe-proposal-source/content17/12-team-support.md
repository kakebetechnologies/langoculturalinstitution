# 12. Team, Governance, Training and Support

*Who does what on both sides, how decisions are made, how the Foundation's people are trained, and how the platform is looked after once it is live.*

| Foundation side | Role in the project | Time needed |
|---|---|---|
| **Executive sponsor** | Champions the project, resolves escalations, approves milestones | A few hours a month |
| **Project coordinator** | Day-to-day decisions, gathers feedback, signs off deliverables | About 4 hours a week |
| **Language committee** and **history and heritage panel** (elders, historians) | Review Leb Lango wording, the dictionary, history, clan and heritage content | Scheduled review sessions |
| **Clan leader liaison**, **communications officer**, **finance officer**, **data protection focal person** | Validate the clan register; supply brand assets and news and become the lead editor; set up giving; handle privacy requests | As needed; the communications officer about 2 days a week after launch |

**Governance.** A monthly steering meeting (sponsor, coordinator, Kakebe lead and CTO), a weekly project call, a demonstration every two weeks and a one-page weekly status report. Issues go first to the coordinator and the Kakebe lead, then to the steering group within three working days. The sponsor approves scope, design and go-live; the coordinator accepts deliverables; the language and history panels own the cultural content; Kakebe owns build, testing and security.

## Training and handover

| Audience | Session | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Administrators** | 1 day, hands-on, in Lira: roles, users, settings, backups, exports | Can manage users, settings and data |
| **Editors and communications** | 1 day: news, pages, events, media, translations, SEO basics | Can publish and maintain content |
| **Moderators and language experts** | 1 day: review queue, dictionary and proverbs, translations | Can review and approve with confidence |
| **Clan leaders and youth ambassadors** | Half day each, in English and Leb Lango: clan self-service; assisted contribution with consent | Can maintain a clan page; can record for an elder |

Materials: an administrator manual, short videos, quick-reference cards in English and Leb Lango, a moderator checklist. Two or three Foundation staff are trained as trainers. At **handover** the Foundation receives the deployment process, access to hosting, domain, database and store accounts in its name, technical and user documentation, a content inventory with the sources behind every loaded fact, and a maintenance runbook.

## Support after launch

**Hypercare is included for the first 90 days:** daily monitoring, rapid fixes at no charge, usage reviews at 30, 60 and 90 days, and training clinics. After that the Foundation chooses a **support plan** (Essential, Standard or Premium) covering uptime monitoring, security updates, daily backups with restore tests, defect fixes, app-store and operating-system updates, and a quarterly review of the roadmap. Response times by severity (critical, high, medium, low) and the availability target are fixed in the support agreement; plan prices are in the Financial Proposal. The Foundation owns its code licence, content, data, domain and store accounts, and can export everything in standard formats at any time, so it stays with Kakebe because the service is good, not because it is locked in.
