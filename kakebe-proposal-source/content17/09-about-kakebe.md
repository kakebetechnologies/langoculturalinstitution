# 9. About Kakebe Technologies

*A youth-led technology company in Lira City that builds software, AI and robotics for real local problems, and trains the next generation to build them too.*

Kakebe Technologies Limited is a youth-led technology hub in **Lira City**, in the heart of Lango. For more than five years we have worked with non-profits, government offices, schools, churches, businesses and community groups across Northern Uganda and beyond. **Mission:** build homegrown software, AI and robotics, and teach young people to build the next ones. **Vision:** a Northern Uganda where youth-led innovation solves local problems and Lira City is known for digital talent.

**Programmes.** *Kakebe Tech Camp* is a residential bootcamp for ages 13 to 30 in robotics, AI, web and app development, entrepreneurship and multimedia; Camp 2025 trained **257** young people from across East Africa, and the next camp runs on 14 to 23 December 2026. The *Digital Bridge Internship Programme* puts interns on live projects with a certificate at the end. The *Innovation Programme*, home to the **Lango Innovation Challenge** and regular Tech Talks, incubates young innovators; several of our own products began there.

## Why Kakebe

**We are local:** our office is in Lira, so workshops with elders, clan leaders and language experts happen in person. **We have already built it:** the demonstrator is a working version of this proposal. **We have proven delivery** of every technology involved: content-managed sites, multi-role admin systems, mobile apps on a shared API, offline-first design, mobile-money payments, tracking codes and government work (Chapter 10). **We build capacity, not dependency:** staff are trained, documentation is complete, and the content and data belong to the Foundation. **We keep the money and the skills in Lango.** **We are accountable:** fixed milestones, a staging site to review at any time, and a named team.
