# 1. Executive Summary

*One platform for the language, clans, history and heritage of the Lango, delivered in three months by a team that has already built a working version.*

{{CLIENT}} holds a heritage older than any archive: Leb Lango, well over a hundred clans, the oral history of nine districts and Lira City, and the ceremonies, songs and proverbs that tie them together. Much of it still lives in elders' memories, family albums and WhatsApp groups, and some is lost every year, while a phone-first, diaspora-connected generation looks for its culture on a screen. Kakebe Technologies proposes to bring the two together with **one platform made of three connected parts**:

| Part | What it is | Who it serves |
|---|---|---|
| **Public website** | A fast, bilingual home for history, districts, clans, heritage, the Language Hub (dictionary, translator, proverbs, lessons), news, events, programmes and giving | Citizens, students, researchers, diaspora, partners, media |
| **Admin control panel** | A role-based workspace where the Foundation's own staff publish, review, translate and report, with no developer needed day to day | Editors, moderators, language experts, clan leaders |
| **Lango Mobile App** | Android, iOS and installable web app: offline dictionary, guided contribution (GPS, camera, voice), notifications, recognition | Youth, clan members, contributors in the field, diaspora |

All three share **one backend, one database and one review queue**. A word suggested on a phone in Aduku and one suggested on a laptop in London land in the same moderation screen, and once an expert approves it, it is live on the website and in the app at the same moment.

> **Our recommendation: build the website, the admin panel and the mobile app together, on one shared platform, and launch all three within three months.**

## Why three months is realistic

We are not starting from a blank page. Kakebe has already built and tested a working demonstrator of this platform for the Lango community (under the working title *Lango Cultural Institution*): more than 30 website page types, 30 managed content types in the admin, about 50 app screens, a documented API, an offline dictionary and an offline contribution queue. The first weeks go into adapting it to the Foundation's identity and filling it with the Foundation's authority, not into discovering what a cultural platform is. Chapters 4 to 6 show it screen by screen.

## What the Foundation gains

- **A trusted public record** of the language, clans, districts and heritage, with sources shown and reviewers named.
- **A Language Hub** that teaches Leb Lango online and offline, and grows through contributions that experts approve.
- **A contribution engine** for words, proverbs, oral histories and sites, captured with GPS, photo and voice, even offline.
- **Transparent giving and programmes**, and **independence**: trained staff, full documentation and no lock-in.
- **A youth skills pipeline**, because the work is done in Lira with trainees from Kakebe's own programmes.
