## Questions for the workshop

1. Who will be the **project coordinator**, and who will sit on the **language committee** and the **history and heritage panel**?
2. What **existing content** is there (registers, dictionaries, histories, photographs, recordings), who owns it, and who can approve it?
3. What are the **cultural protocols** for restricted knowledge, recordings of elders and photographs of children?
4. What **identity** will the platform carry (name, logo, colours, domain), and how should **leadership and officeholders** be presented?
5. Which **recognition features** (points, levels, leaderboard) should be public, and is **live payment** wanted at launch or later?
6. Which **dates** (festivals, anniversaries, funding deadlines) should the launch align with, and which partners or institutions should be informed?
