## Three references in more detail

**FaithFellows: the Lango App's architecture, in production.** One Laravel backend serves church websites and an Expo / React Native app through the same API, in English, Luo, Luganda and Swahili, with an installable web app, a lite mode for slow connections, offline listening, push notifications and moderated community features: the same combination this platform needs.

**SignConnect Uganda: a community's language, taught on a phone.** Short tagged sign videos with slow motion and repeat, captions, lessons, classes with join codes, parent profiles and progress, bulk upload for admins, designed to WCAG 2.1 AA. It is the closest cousin to the Language Hub.

**Lira City website and iRecover: tracking codes and review workflows.** Citizens report an issue or a lost document, receive a tracking code and follow it through staff review: the pattern the contribution engine reuses for the Foundation.
