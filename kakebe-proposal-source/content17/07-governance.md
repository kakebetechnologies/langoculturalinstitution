# 7. Contribution and Content Governance

*How knowledge enters the archive, who decides what is true, and how the platform protects the Foundation's credibility and its people.*

> **The platform never invents culture.** Clans, proverbs, translations, biographies and history come only from the Foundation, from published sources shown on the page, or from community contributions that an authorised reviewer has approved. Where something is missing, the platform shows it as empty, not made up.

![fig|Figure 7.1: The lifecycle of a contribution, from capture on the phone or website to a published, attributed archive record.](figures/fig-workflow.svg)

A contribution moves through six statuses the contributor can see: *Pending*, *Under review*, *Returned* (with the reviewer's note), *Approved*, *Published* or *Rejected* (with a reason). Anyone can check a submission by its tracking code without logging in. **Moderators** (language experts and community managers) review; **clan leaders** edit only their own clan and cannot publish their own changes; **editors** publish Foundation content; **admins** manage users. ## Every record says how far to trust it

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | Checked and approved by the Foundation's experts |
| **Community** | Contributed by a member and approved by a moderator, not yet verified |
| **Unverified** | Loaded from a starter source (for example the starter dictionary) and awaiting expert review |
| **Source cited** | History, districts and people show the published sources they come from |

## Cultural protocols (to be agreed at discovery)

| Protocol | How the platform supports it |
|---|---|
| **Informed consent** | Recording and photo forms record that the speaker or owner agreed to the use of their words and image |
| **Attribution** | Credit by name, by clan or anonymously, as the contributor wishes |
| **Restricted knowledge** | A record can be held in the archive but shown only to authorised roles, or not at all |
| **Correction and removal** | Every page will offer a route to request a correction or withdrawal, handled in the review queue |
| **Children's content** | Clear rules and guardian consent for photographs and recordings of children |
| **Neutrality** | Leadership titles and officeholders appear exactly as the Foundation confirms; the platform takes no position on disputes |

**Ownership.** The Foundation owns the content and data; contributors keep authorship and grant a licence to publish and preserve. Public reuse as open data is the Foundation's choice. **Abuse controls:** rate limits, duplicate checks, moderation before publication, account controls. **The Cultural Assistant** answers only from the register and reviewed archive, shows its sources, and logs what it cannot answer so the Foundation can fill the gap.
