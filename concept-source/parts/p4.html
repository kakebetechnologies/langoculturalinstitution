How it works

Communities write it. Experts review it. Everyone can use it.

Knowledge moves through a simple loop, and at every step the community stays in charge of what is said about it.

  1. ShareA member adds a word, photo, story, proverb or clan history in the app or on the website. It is saved on the phone and sent when there is a connection.
  2. ReviewLanguage experts, clan leaders and moderators check it in the admin system and approve, return or decline it. Nothing is called verified until a reviewer says so.
  3. PublishApproved material appears in the app, the website and the open API, with its credit and its status.
  4. Learn and useLearners, schools, the diaspora, researchers and planners use it. Points, streaks and badges reward contributors.
  5. ImproveWhat people search for and cannot find shows where to collect next.

Trust is built in

  • Community-owned. Content is shared with consent and the community keeps its rights to what it shares.
  • Expert-verified. A reviewer decides what is published, and “verified” is shown only when a reviewer has marked it so.
  • Credited. Photographs, sources and contributions carry credit lines, and contributors choose whether their name is shown.
  • Private and safe. Members sign in, the location data inside photos is removed, and a member can ask to delete their account and data.
