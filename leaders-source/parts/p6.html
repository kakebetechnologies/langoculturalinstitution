Where we are

Built, and being tested now. The phone apps are not yet in the stores.

The app, the website and the review system are working. The phone apps are being tested by a small group and are not yet in the app stores; the website at myculture.online is already open to anyone. We are showing it to you before the apps are released, because Lango leaders should shape it, not meet it afterwards. Tell us what should be changed, held back or withdrawn, and we will act on it.

Almost all of it comes from published sources. No clan, heritage entry or proverb has been marked verified; one dictionary word, a test entry, is. Two clans have a totem named so far, and 20 of the 30 heritage entries name their source.

What is not ready, said plainly

  • The Leb Lango wording of the app itself (it is in English today).
  • Whole-sentence translation: the translator handles single words and set phrases.
  • Recordings and example sentences for the words.
  • Expert verification of words, clans and heritage.
  • Clan leaders’ names and photos on the clan pages, and a check of who belongs to which clan.
  • Elders’ stories, to be gathered with consent, and release in the app stores.
