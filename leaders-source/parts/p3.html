What MyCulture is

One place for the language, the clans and the heritage of Lango.

MyCulture is a free phone app (for Android and iPhone, in testing) and a website at myculture.online. Anyone can learn from it. Members can add what they know, and a reviewer checks each contribution before it is shown. Every screen below is the real app as it is today.

{{ICON:book}}

Learn the language

Dictionary, word translator, 17 proverbs, a phrasebook of greetings, a word every day and quizzes.

{{ICON:users}}

Know your people

25 clans listed so far, districts, notable Langos, the story of the Lango, a timeline and heritage sites.

{{ICON:headphones}}

Listen and read

The Lango Bible of the Bible Society of Uganda opens inside the app. Places for radio and elders’ stories are ready.

{{ICON:camera}}

Add what you know

A word, a proverb, a story, a photo or a recording, even without internet, and follow it through review.

Made to be trusted and easy to share

  • Free to use, with no advertising and no selling of anyone’s personal data. Giving to help keep it running is optional.
  • Sources and credits are named where they are known: most heritage entries name their source, and a photo’s credit appears where it is known.
  • The dictionary works without internet once saved from Settings, and contributions wait until the phone is connected.
  • Clan groups: each clan has a group for signed-in members who say they belong to it. Messages can be reported (three reports hide one), and moderators and the clan’s leader can remove messages. Belonging is not yet checked by the Awitong.
  • A person can delete their account at any time in the app.
