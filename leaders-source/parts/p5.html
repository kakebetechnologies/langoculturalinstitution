Your authority

MyCulture does not decide what is Lango. You do.

The platform gives Lango people the tools to check and correct. This is how the marks work today, and what we propose so that the final word is yours.

Step 1 · shown with its source

Published

Shown only after an editor or reviewer has published it, with its source and, where known, its credit.
Step 2 · the mark on clans

Awaiting verification

Every clan page carries this mark now, because no one has checked them yet. Other entries show a Verified mark only once someone has checked them.
Step 3 · your decision

Verified

Set today by an editor or administrator. We propose that the editors are people you name, and that nothing is marked without the confirmation of a clan leader or language expert.

Built and working now Built

  • Review before showing: a contribution from a member is read by a reviewer first. Today a reviewer’s approval publishes a word at once.
  • An account for each Awitong (a clan leader), in the website’s admin panel: edit the clan’s page (changes show at once and earlier versions are kept), see the clan’s contributions, with contact details, and approve or decline them. Only an editor can publish a new page or mark it verified.
  • Credit by choice for words and proverbs; for photos, stories and recordings the app asks how they may be used.
  • Delete my account at any time; no advertising; no selling of personal data; sources named on most heritage entries.

What we propose, to shape with you Proposed

  • A Lango review team that you name: language elders for words and proverbs, and a leader for each clan to confirm its page. Each clan decides who speaks for it; where a clan is divided we will not choose.
  • Rules for sensitive knowledge: what is public, what is for the clan only, what is not recorded, and whether clans should be ranked against each other at all (the app has a clan leaderboard today).
  • Consent for recordings: a simple form so elders agree how their words and photos are used.
  • Leb Lango wording for the app, written by language experts.
  • Terms to agree with you: our principle is that the knowledge belongs to the Lango people. Today contributors keep their rights and published items are open to reuse with credit (CC BY 4.0); the real terms are agreed with you before release.

Our promises

  • We will never sell personal data or show advertising.
  • Lango knowledge will not be given or licensed to outside parties, such as researchers or AI developers, without the agreement of the Lango leaders you name.
  • What is live now comes from published sources such as Driberg’s 1923 book. Tell us what should be withdrawn and we will take it down.
  • Where we do not know, the app says so; we do not guess. We will report plainly what has been checked and what has not.
